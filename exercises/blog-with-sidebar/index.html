My Iris

My Louis

If I were to be asked who's my "iris", or to whom I dedicate the song "iris" by Goo Goo Dolls, I'd certainly answer Louis - no doubt, no hesitation, no second thoughts, and in a heartbeat.

As a person who fears to be shown raw to people and have its soul and heart be seen, as a person who doesn't want the world to see her because of doubt being misunderstood by people, as a person who thinks everything was made to be broken- I am very wholeheartedly loving and expressive when it comes to him. And also, as a person who yearns and desires to feel and experience life and love entirely, Louis is the one who made me experience it at the same time, without any conditions, even without having to sacrifice anything.

Louis is my Iris— the one who shows the beauty and colors of life right in my eyes.

We may be far from each other, but loving him is the closest to heaven that I'll ever be and l'd give up forever to see and touch him.

Author: Kinsei Natsumi

Published: May 2025
